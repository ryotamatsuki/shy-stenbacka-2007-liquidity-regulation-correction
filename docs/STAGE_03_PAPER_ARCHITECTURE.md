# Stage 3 — Candidate Architecture

## A — Minimal derivative correction
Scope: Eq. (7)→Eq. (8), Eqs. (11)–(12), Proposition 1(c).
Assessment: too small after the independent welfare audit uncovered a separate constrained-planner problem.

## B — Reduced-equilibrium correction and regulatory reassessment
Scope:
- corrected derivative, Hessian and reduced best response;
- corrected symmetric rate and fee;
- Proposition 1 comparative statics;
- reserve clipping;
- δ=0 and δ=1 endpoints;
- complete v=r equality case;
- welfare reconstruction;
- correct constrained policy correspondence;
- implementation/scope separation.

Assessment: **SELECTED.** This is the minimal architecture that contains every material independently verified downstream consequence without inventing a new banking model.

## C — Full unrestricted two-instrument banking-game reconstruction
Scope: B plus complete asymmetric clipped/off-path equilibrium correspondence.
Assessment: rejected for this paper. The source does not fully specify market-share clipping, asymmetric reserve feedback and tie breaking after large deviations. Completing those rules would be a model extension rather than a correction.
